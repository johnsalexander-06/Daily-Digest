Air & Space Forces Magazine · Aug 5

US has burned through most of its high-end missile interceptors

The U.S. military has expended nearly four-fifths of its THAAD interceptor inventory and roughly half of its Patriot stocks since the start of the war with Iran, with commanders privately warning that stockpiles are "dangerously low." The drawdown concentrates in exactly the high-end systems needed to defend fixed sites and allied capitals against ballistic threats. At current production rates, replenishment runs years behind expenditure.

So What: Deep interceptor magazines can no longer be assumed for layered air-and-missile defense — refine defended-asset lists now, plan for interceptor rationing, and press for accelerated FY27 munitions buys.

ISW — Russian Offensive Campaign Assessment · Aug 4

Ukraine's 40-day deep-strike campaign is degrading Russia's war economy

Ukraine's SBU says it conducted more than 100 successful deep strikes in the first 40 days of a campaign begun June 26, hitting airbases in Crimea and Russia, a Tu-95MS bomber, Su-35 fighters, 14 oil refineries, warships, and shadow-fleet tankers. Combined with broader Ukrainian strikes, the effort has cut Russian refining capacity by at least a third and forced near-record crude exports, per Bloomberg data cited by ISW. Zelensky has approved a follow-on operation.

So What: Deep-strike attrition is now a sustained campaign imposing real economic cost on Moscow — NATO planners should study the drone-strike model and anticipate Russian retaliation and air-defense reprioritization.

US and Iran near a deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz

The U.S. and Iran could reach a deal as soon as today to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and de-escalate, with Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent saying talks are active and that "freedom of movement" is the goal; Qatar and Oman are mediating. So What: A reopening would relieve energy and shipping pressure but could collapse without warning — hold escort and force-protection postures until a durable, verified agreement is in place.
